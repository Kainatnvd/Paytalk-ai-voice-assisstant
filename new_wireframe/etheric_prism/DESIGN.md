# Design System Specification: The Ethereal Professional

## 1. Overview & Creative North Star: "The Digital Curator"
This design system rejects the "boxed-in" nature of traditional web interfaces. Our North Star, **The Digital Curator**, treats the screen as an open, airy gallery space. We achieve a premium feel not through heavy ornamentation, but through intentional white space, sophisticated tonal layering, and "frosted" glass elements that suggest depth without clutter.

To break the "template" look, designers must embrace **intentional asymmetry**. Align text to a rigorous grid, but allow imagery and glass containers to bleed off-canvas or overlap sections. This system is designed to feel "bespoke editorial"—it should look like it was laid out by hand, not generated by a framework.

---

## 2. Colors & Surface Philosophy
The palette is rooted in a pure white base, punctuated by a high-intellect purplish-blue and deep indigo neutrals.

### Surface Hierarchy & Nesting
We achieve depth through **Tonal Layering** rather than lines.
- **Base Layer:** Use `surface` (#f5f7f9) for the global background.
- **Sectioning:** Use `surface-container-low` (#eef1f3) to define large content blocks.
- **Prominence:** Use `surface-container-lowest` (#ffffff) for the highest-priority cards or interactive zones.

### The "No-Line" Rule
**Explicit Instruction:** Prohibit the use of 1px solid borders for sectioning. Boundaries must be defined solely through background color shifts or subtle tonal transitions. A `surface-container-low` card sitting on a `surface` background provides all the definition a sophisticated eye needs.

### The "Glass & Gradient" Rule
To elevate beyond the "standard" light mode:
- **Glassmorphism:** For floating navigation bars or modal overlays, use `surface` at 70% opacity with a `24px` backdrop-blur. 
- **Signature Textures:** For primary CTAs, do not use flat hex codes. Apply a subtle linear gradient from `primary` (#4647d3) to `primary-container` (#9396ff) at a 135-degree angle. This adds a "visual soul" and a sense of light hitting the element.

---

## 3. Typography: Editorial Authority
We use **Inter** for its neutral, high-legibility architecture. The key to this system is the high-contrast scale.

*   **Display (Large/Medium):** Used for "Hero" moments. Tighten letter-spacing by `-0.02em` to create a compact, authoritative "Logotype" feel.
*   **Headlines:** These are your anchors. Use `headline-lg` (2rem) to lead the eye, ensuring ample `margin-bottom` (at least 2.4rem) to let the typography breathe.
*   **Body:** `body-lg` (1rem) is our workhorse. Use a generous line-height (1.6) to maintain the "Airy" requirement.
*   **Labels:** `label-sm` (0.6875rem) should always be uppercase with `+0.05em` letter-spacing when used for category tags or metadata.

---

## 4. Elevation & Depth: The Layering Principle
Shadows in this system are "Ambient," not "Structural."

*   **Shadow Construction:** Shadows must be extra-diffused. Use a blur radius of 30px–60px with a low-opacity (4%-8%) tint of `on-surface` (#2c2f31). Never use pure black (#000).
*   **The "Ghost Border" Fallback:** If accessibility requires a container edge, use a "Ghost Border": the `outline-variant` token (#abadaf) at **15% opacity**. It should be felt, not seen.
*   **Frosted Depth:** When stacking glass elements, the `surface-tint` (#4647d3) can be applied at 2% opacity to the glass layer to give it a slight "cool" chromatic lift, separating it from the warm white base.

---

## 5. Components

### Buttons & Interaction
*   **Primary:** Gradient fill (`primary` to `primary-container`), `md` (0.75rem) roundedness, and a soft `primary` glow shadow on hover.
*   **Secondary:** No fill. Use a `surface-container-high` background on hover.
*   **Tertiary:** Text-only, using `primary` color with an underline that only appears on hover.

### Cards & Lists
*   **Zero-Border Policy:** Cards must use `surface-container-lowest` and an ambient shadow. 
*   **Spacing over Dividers:** Forbid the use of divider lines in lists. Use `1.5rem` of vertical white space to separate list items. If separation is critical, use a subtle background shift to `surface-container-low` on every other item.

### Input Fields
*   **Stateful Design:** Inputs use `surface-container-lowest` with a `Ghost Border`. Upon focus, the border disappears and is replaced by a 2px `primary` bottom-glow and a subtle elevation lift.

### Glass Navigation (Signature Component)
*   **The Floating Dock:** Instead of a top-pinned header, use a floating navigation bar with `full` (9999px) roundedness, a glassmorphic background, and `24px` backdrop blur. Position it 2rem from the top of the viewport.

---

## 6. Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
*   **Do** use asymmetrical padding (e.g., more padding on the left than the right in a hero section) to create a custom, editorial feel.
*   **Do** leverage "White Space as a Feature." If a layout feels "off," add 20% more padding before changing colors.
*   **Do** use `primary-container` for subtle highlights behind icons to create a "soft focus" effect.

### Don't:
*   **Don't** use 100% opaque, high-contrast borders. They "kill" the airy, premium aesthetic.
*   **Don't** use pure black for text. Always use `on-surface` (#2c2f31) to maintain a soft, sophisticated contrast.
*   **Don't** crowd the "Frosted Glass" elements. Glass needs a colorful or textured background underneath to be visually effective; don't place glass on a plain white background.
